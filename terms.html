4. What the app does to your files

Performance King changes files on your phone. It reads, copies, moves, packs, checks and deletes files in your phone's shared storage. In particular:

  • It packs files into a smaller form and keeps them in a hidden folder named ".PerformanceKing" in your phone's main storage. You need Performance King to put a packed file back.

  • It copies files to backup places you choose, such as a memory card, a USB drive, or a folder offered by another app.
