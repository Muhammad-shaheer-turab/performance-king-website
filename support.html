Help

Where do removed files go?

Into the recycle bin, inside the app, in the folders they came from. Open Recycle bin, tick the files and tap Put back to return them to where they were.

How do I get a packed file back?

Open Pack away files, then Packed away. Tick the files and tap Put back. They return to their folders exactly as they were, byte for byte.

Why will it not delete some files in the bin?

Because they are the only copy. Performance King never deletes the last copy of a file. If you really want one gone, put it back and delete it with your phone’s own Files app, at your own risk.

What should I do before uninstalling?

Put back everything you packed (Pack away files, then Packed away, then Put back). Uninstalling does not delete the hidden “.PerformanceKing” folder, but you need the app to put packed files back. Reinstalling it lets you get them back later.

A cleaner app deleted the “.PerformanceKing” folder. Can I get my files back?

Only from your own backup. That folder holds the packed files and the recycle bin; anything deleted with it is gone. This is why you should always keep a backup away from the phone.

Why does it need “All files access”?

To find duplicate copies and large files in every folder, pack them, and put them back where they were. Android offers no narrower permission that allows this. The app has no internet permission, so nothing it reads can leave your phone.

Does it upload my files?

No. It cannot use the internet. Files leave your phone only when you back them up to a place you chose, or send them to another app yourself.

Will it make my phone faster?

It frees storage space. A phone that is completely full often works badly, and space helps, but Performance King makes no promise about speed.

Still stuck?

Write to the email address shown in the "App support" section of Performance King's Google Play page. Say which phone you have and what you tapped. Never send passwords or private files.
