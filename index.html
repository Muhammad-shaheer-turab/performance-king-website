Make room on a full phone.

Performance King finds what fills your phone and frees space carefully. It packs files smaller without changing a byte inside them, clears out spare copies of the same photo, and keeps whatever it removes in a recycle bin you can put back from.

Get it on Google Play

Free. No ads. No account. No internet permission. Android 11 and newer.

What it does

Pack away files

Documents, photos and other files are stored in less space and come back exactly as they were, byte for byte, when you put them back.

Clear duplicates

Finds identical copies of the same file in different folders, keeps one, and moves the spares to the recycle bin.

Recycle bin

Everything the app removes waits here, in the folders it came from, until you put it back or free the spare copies.

Back up

Copies your files as ordinary files to a memory card, a USB drive or a folder you pick, so they open anywhere.

How it treats your files

Never the last copy

It is built never to delete the only copy of a file. It frees a spare only after checking another copy exists and reads back correctly.

Nothing leaves your phone

It has no permission to use the internet and collects nothing about you. Files leave only when you back them up or send them yourself.

Made for small phones

It works on phones with little memory and little space left, and keeps going if you switch to another app.

Read this before you use it

Like any app, Performance King can fail, and phones and memory cards fail too. You could lose files. Keep your own backup of everything that matters, away from your phone.
