About Performance King

Performance King is made by an independent developer, named on the app's Google Play page, for people whose phones are always full: cheap phones with little storage, where every photo has to fight for room.

The rules it is built by

  • Never the last copy. The app is built never to delete the only copy of a file. There is no button that could.
  • Check before freeing. Before it frees anything, it reads back the copy that stays and makes sure it matches.
  • Undo where possible. Whatever it removes waits in a recycle bin, in the folders it came from, until you decide.
  • Plain words. Every screen says what will happen, to which files, before it happens.
  • Nothing collected. No accounts, no ads, no analytics and no internet permission.

What it is not

It is not a "booster" and does not claim to make your phone faster. It frees storage space, and only in ways that can be checked.

Like any software it can fail. Keep your own backups, and read the Terms of Use before using it.
