Performance King — Privacy Policy

Effective 1 October 2026 · Version 2026-10-01

In short: Performance King collects nothing about you. It has no permission to use the internet, so it cannot send your files, or anything about them, to us or to anyone else. Everything it does happens on your phone.

1. Who is responsible

This policy is for the app "Performance King", published by the individual developer named on its Google Play page ("we", "us"). It covers every edition of the app.

2. What we collect: nothing

We do not collect, receive, store, sell, rent or share any personal data, or any other data, from the app. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics, no tracking, no crash reporting of its own and no internet permission; Android itself stops an app without that permission from connecting to the internet.

3. What the app reads and keeps on your phone

To do its job the app works with the following, only on your phone:

  • Your files. With the "All files access" permission it reads the names, sizes, dates and contents of files in your phone's shared storage, to show what takes space, find identical copies by working out a fingerprint of each file's contents, pack files, check that copies are intact, and back files up and put them back.
  • What it keeps beside your files. Packed files, the recycle bin, and records of each file's original name, folder, size, date and fingerprint, in a hidden folder named ".PerformanceKing" in your phone's main storage. Other apps with full storage access, and anyone who can get into your phone, can read this folder. It is not encrypted.
  • What it keeps in its own storage. Your settings, the backup places you chose and what was copied there, records that let an interrupted job finish safely, small pictures of your files for its lists, and when you agreed to these documents.
  • Apps on your phone. The full edition, which is not on Google Play, reads the list of installed apps and their sizes, to show which take space and to remove or reinstall them when you ask.

None of this leaves your phone through the app.

4. When files leave your phone because you chose so

  • Backups. When you back up, the app writes copies of your files to the place you picked: a memory card, a USB drive, or a folder offered by another app. If that folder belongs to a cloud storage app, such as Google Drive, that app, not Performance King, uploads the files, under its own privacy policy.
  • Sending files to an app. When you send or share files, they go to the app you choose, under that app's own policy.

We never receive these files.

5. Google Play and Android

When you install the app from Google Play, Google processes information under its own privacy policy. If you have agreed with Google to share usage and diagnostics, Google may give us statistics such as the number of installs and anonymous crash reports. These do not identify you, and we do not use them to identify anyone.

6. Children

The app is meant for adults aged 18 and over and is not directed at children. It collects nothing from anyone, children included.

7. Your choices and rights

We hold no data about you, so there is nothing for us to show you, correct, hand over or delete. Privacy laws such as the GDPR in the EU and the UK, or the CCPA in California, give you rights over data a company holds about you; we hold none. You control everything the app keeps on your phone: put back your packed files, then uninstall the app and delete the ".PerformanceKing" folder. Deleting that folder before putting files back deletes the packed files for good.

8. Security

Your files stay on your phone and in the backup places you choose. The app does not encrypt them. Protect your phone with a screen lock, and keep your backups somewhere safe.

9. This website

The app's website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub receives your IP address and browser details when you visit, under GitHub's own privacy statement. The website itself sets no cookies, loads nothing from other sites and uses no analytics.

10. Changes

If this policy changes, the app shows you the new version and asks you to agree before you continue, and the website shows the new date.

11. Contact

Write to the developer at the email address shown in the "App support" section of Performance King's Google Play page.
